# Only commercials and promos make up a commercial break

A commercial break holds only advertising spots: commercials (from a program's sponsors, other advertisers, or public-service campaigns such as ACジャパン) and promos for the channel's other programs, events or films. Sponsor credits, station IDs, next-episode previews, in-program advertising (「PR」 segments, product placement) and whole-slot shopping programs all count as program. This keeps the rule simple and avoids commercial breaks only a few seconds long, such as a lone sponsor credit, that no detector could catch in time; promos stay in because they sit among commercials and look like them, and telling the two apart would mean identifying what is advertised (see ADR 0001).

## Consequences

Every label depends on this rule, so changing it later means relabeling every recording.
